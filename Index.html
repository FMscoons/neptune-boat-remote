Interactive prototype · Smart boat lift control

Boat Remote + Lift Simulator

Press UP, STOP or DOWN on the phone. The boat moves until you press STOP or it reaches a limit. In Manual mode the boat moves only while you hold UP or DOWN; tap AUTO/MANUAL to let it run on its own. LIGHT switches the pole light. Keyboard: hold arrows, space to stop, A for auto, L for light.
